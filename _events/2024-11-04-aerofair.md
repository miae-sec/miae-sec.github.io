The 2024 AeroFair organized was networking event bringing together industry leaders including Airbus, Pratt and Whitney, Cert Center Canada, Héroux Devtek, MDA, Bell, and more! This was a great opportunity to connect with top professionals from the aerospace industry. Industry professionals were also giving presentations about the aerospace industry. Over 150 students showed up to gain valuable insights into the aerospace field.
