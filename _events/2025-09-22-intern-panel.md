Landing a first internship in the aerospace world can feel like a daunting climb, which is why we prompted our student community to come together for an evening of honest conversation and career preparation. We were thrilled to see about 30 motivated students take the initiative to join us in the MacDonald Engineering Building, all eager to sharpen their edge before the McGill Tech Fair. The evening started with a practical workshop led by Elizaveta Vorontsova from the Engineering Career Centre, who helped us demystify the recruitment process. Instead of just general advice, students received hands-on tips on how to polish their resumes and master the soft skills that turn a quick introduction into a lasting impression with a recruiter.
