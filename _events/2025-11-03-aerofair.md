The McGill Aero Fair was more than just a formal gathering/networking event; it was a vibrant platform showcasing the passion and ambition that define our aerospace community. Driven by the tireless initiative of the MIAE student executive committee and the McGill Vertical Flight Society, the fair transformed our campus into a high-energy hub where the future of flight felt within reach. Under the dedicated guidance of Professor Sivakumaran Nadarajah, our student organizers worked behind the scenes to bring twelve global titans—including Airbus, MDA Space, Bombardier, and Pratt & Whitney—to the heart of McGill. The atmosphere was electric as 191 students from across Engineering and Computer Science engaged in genuine, face-to-face conversations. The impact of the day resided in these personal interactions, as students handed over their CVs and shared their project experiences directly with industry leaders. We are incredibly proud of the MIAE team for fostering an environment where talent meets opportunity, proving that when you bring the right people together, the possibilities are endless.
