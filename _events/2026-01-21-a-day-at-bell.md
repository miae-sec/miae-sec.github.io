Bell Textron Canada is a vertical lift company focused on innovation, customer experience and superior service and support for customers around the globe. From engineering to finances and supply chain, the company is represented by more than a thousand high-skilled employees working in various aspects of the business.
During this micro-WIL, students got an overview of the different career paths possible in the company, as well as the opportunity to visit the Mirabel assembly plant. They also participated in an open discussion about the future of vertical lift, as well as networked with different managers and technical experts at the end of the micro-WIL.
