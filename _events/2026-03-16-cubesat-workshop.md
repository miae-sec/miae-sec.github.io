What if we told you McGill could launch a satellite into space? 🚀

Students joined us to learn more about satellites and challenges in space engineering at our CubeSat Workshop! They got the chance to learn more about what a CubeSat is and how McGill Rocket Team is building McGill's first satellite! They also got the chance to learn how they can get involved in McGill's first space mission.

We were also be joined by Yianni Hudon-Castillo, a professional from MDA Space, to learn more about challenges in the space industry.
