On March 28, the MIAE took over MD267 for the Fly-By-Wire Challenge with Airbus. Participants built custom wing sections with active flap systems, utilizing Arduino kits generously provided by Professor Sabih. The mission was to code a control loop that could survive our simulated flight profile—proving that software engineering is just as vital to flight as the airfoils themselves.
We were honored to have two experts from Airbus on-site to mentor the teams, following a deep dive into the physics of lift by Professor Nadarajah. Every team rose to the occasion, successfully navigating "turbulence" and "gusts" to earn their certificates of completion. It was a high-energy success that showcased the incredible talent and "flight-ready" logic within our McGill aerospace community.
