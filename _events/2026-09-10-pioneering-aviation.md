Join us for an evening with Ashraf Mostafa, Systems Test Lead at Joby Aviation and McGill alumnus, as he shares insights from more than 26 years in the aerospace industry.

Ashraf will discuss his career journey, aircraft testing and certification, the challenges behind bringing new aircraft technologies to market, and the rapidly evolving world of eVTOL and Advanced Air Mobility.

This is a great opportunity for students interested in aerospace, engineering, emerging aviation technologies, or careers in the industry to hear directly from an experienced aerospace leader.
