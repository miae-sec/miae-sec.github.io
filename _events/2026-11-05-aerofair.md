McGill's only student-focused aerospace career fair is back! AeroFair 2026, presented by Pratt & Whitney and Calian, brings leading aerospace companies from across Montreal together with engineering students for a day of live presentations, booths, and one-on-one conversations with the people hiring.

Bring a CV and come curious: most of what happens at AeroFair happens in conversation.
