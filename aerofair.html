Register for Aerofair

One room, the whole industry

Aerofair brings leading aerospace companies from across Montreal together with engineering students from McGill and other Montreal universities for a day of live presentations, booths, and one-on-one conversations between the people hiring and the students who want to build what comes next.

What to expect

For students

Direct access to leading aerospace companies, live presentations, and one-on-one networking with representatives about careers and internships. Bring a CV and come curious: most of what happens at Aerofair happens in conversation.

Register now

For companies

Direct access to engineering talent from across Montréal, with a platform to present and connect with future hires early.
